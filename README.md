# Climate Health Risk Predictor

Machine Learning-Powered Climate-Influenced Health Risks

## Problem Statement

Climate conditions can shape health risks and mortality, especially among vulnerable communities. Yet, identifying these climate-influenced health outcomes from complex health, demographic, geographic, and environmental data remains a challenge.

This challenge aims to leverage **machine learning to predict climate-sensitive mortality**, uncover key risk patterns, and provide data-driven insights into how environmental conditions may influence health outcomes.
